# Improvement roadmap

This roadmap describes areas for continued product and engineering improvement. It is not a guarantee or delivery schedule.

## Near term

- Reduce the amount of catalogue content sent on initial page load and progressively reveal additional items.
- Improve responsive image sizing and review large image and video assets.
- Measure page performance on representative mobile devices and networks in Bangladesh.
- Continue improving content editor clarity and shared media metadata workflows.

## Ongoing

- Maintain clear, accurate product details for purchase, rental, refill, warranty, and delivery.
- Review accessibility and language quality as new pages and products are published.
- Monitor route health, search discoverability, and the customer enquiry journey.
- Keep dependency updates, security review, backup verification, and release validation on a regular cadence.

## Longer term

- Strengthen real-user performance monitoring and page-level quality reporting.
- Improve content governance for product and medical-information updates.
- Refine catalogue navigation as the product range expands.
- Evaluate customer-service improvements using feedback and operational evidence.
