# Quality, discoverability, and performance

## Search discoverability

The public experience is designed around useful, indexable product and category pages. Search metadata, canonical URLs, page titles and descriptions, structured data, image descriptions, and sitemap coverage support search engines in understanding the catalogue. Content quality and accurate product information remain essential; technical SEO does not guarantee search rankings.

## Accessibility

Product images should have meaningful alternative text when they convey information. Interactive controls should remain understandable and usable with keyboard input and assistive technology. Headings, labels, focus visibility, and readable contrast are important across both language experiences.

## Performance goals

Performance work focuses on limiting unnecessary page content, reducing client-side work, serving appropriately sized media, and measuring real mobile experience. Improvements should be validated across home, shop, category, product, and article pages rather than inferred from one route.

Useful field targets follow the current Core Web Vitals guidance: Largest Contentful Paint at or below 2.5 seconds, Interaction to Next Paint at or below 200 milliseconds, and Cumulative Layout Shift at or below 0.1 for at least 75% of visits. These are goals for measurement, not claims of achieved scores.

## Medical information

Product descriptions and educational content are general information. Equipment selection, therapy decisions, settings, and patient care should be guided by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional and manufacturer instructions.

## Quality checks

Before publishing significant updates, review:

1. Mobile and desktop layout at common viewport sizes.
2. Product/category navigation, search, filters, and direct page links.
3. Current price, stock, rental/refill, and delivery wording.
4. English and Bengali text, metadata, and image alternatives.
5. Cart, enquiry, support, and contact routes.
6. Canonical URLs, structured data, and sitemap coverage.
